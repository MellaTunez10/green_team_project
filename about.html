Who We Are

Reliable Solar Power Built for Nigeria

SunGrid Energy Nigeria is a renewable energy company based in Abuja, focused on helping homes, businesses and organisations take control of their energy needs through dependable solar power solutions.

We understand the challenges that unreliable electricity can create for families and businesses. Our approach combines quality solar technology, professional installation and practical energy planning to deliver systems designed around the actual needs of our customers.

From residential solar installations and battery storage to commercial energy systems and maintenance, we make the transition to clean energy simple, practical and sustainable.

Our Approach

Solar Energy Designed Around You

Every property has different energy requirements. That's why we don't believe in one-size-fits-all solar systems.

We begin by understanding your energy consumption, available space and power requirements. We then recommend a solution that balances performance, reliability and long-term value.

  1. Assess your energy requirements and current power usage.
  2. Design a solar system suited to your property and budget.
  3. Install quality equipment using professional installation practices.
  4. Support your system with maintenance and ongoing technical assistance.
